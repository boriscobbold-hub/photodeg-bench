# Limitations

1. **Access, the largest limitation.** 54 papers that meet the criteria on their abstracts (75% of all in-scope papers) had no full text retrievable by this pipeline: they are not in Europe PMC's open-access XML, and publisher sites were not reachable from the build environment. They include central studies on visible-light depolymerization of commercial polymethacrylates, PET-RAFT depolymerization, poly(vinyl ether) upcycling and several polystyrene photo-oxidation systems. v1.0 is therefore a verified sample of the open literature, biased toward journals that deposit in PubMed Central, not a census. They are listed (code E6) in `data/curated/screening_log.csv` for v1.1.
2. **AI curation.** Screening, extraction, verification and harmonization decisions were made by an AI system under the author's delegation; no human expert reviewed individual records. Deterministic checks guarantee that each number is printed at its cited location; correct attribution to the experiment relies on independent AI re-extraction and adjudication.
3. **Text and tables only.** Values shown only in figures or supplementary files were not recorded (109 observations listed with reasons). Molar-mass time courses, in particular, are mostly in figures.
4. **Small and uneven.** 87 records from 10 papers; polystyrene dominates (polystyrene 48, polypinacol 12, poly(benzyl methacrylate) 8, poly(ethylene terephthalate) 7, poly(methyl methacrylate) 6, polypropylene 3, poly(styrene-co-acrylonitrile) 1, poly(acrylonitrile-co-butadiene-co-styrene) 1, poly(methyl methacrylate-co-glycidyl methacrylate) 1).
5. **Conventional SEC is relative.** Calibration classes: not_applicable 58, unknown 17, mismatched_conventional 8, matched_conventional 4. Absolute cross-paper comparison of molar masses is approximate.
6. **Light dose not harmonized.** Most papers report electrical lamp power or colour, not irradiance or photon flux at the sample.
7. **Heat as co-driver.** Thermal regimes: not_reported 44, ambient 24, high_temperature 16, heated 3. In at least one system a dark control at the same temperature gives most of the lit result; such records are flagged.
8. **Approximate and borrowed values.** QA flags: approximate_value 35, heated_ge_80C 19, standard_conditions_applied 33, thermal_contribution 15, bimodal_sec 2, sec_estimate 13. Values printed as approximate are recorded as printed and flagged; conditions taken from a paper's own "standard conditions" statement are flagged.
9. **Publication bias.** Positive results are reported more than failures; controls are kept but the benchmark over-represents successful conditions.
10. **Scope conventions.** Photothermal routes and photoreforming after chemical pretreatment were excluded (E4); this is escalated to the author and may change in later versions.
